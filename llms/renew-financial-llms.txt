# Renew Financial

> Renew Financial (Renew Financial Group LLC) is a specialty consumer finance company founded in 2008 and headquartered in Oakland, California. It pioneered and administers Property Assessed Clean Energy (PACE) financing for residential energy efficiency, renewable energy, water efficiency and resiliency home improvements, running the CaliforniaFIRST program in California and RenewPACE in Florida. It is licensed by the California Department of Financial Protection and Innovation as a PACE Program Administrator (#60DBO-90653) and carries NMLS ID 1788120.

Renew Financial does NOT publish a public API, developer portal, API reference, SDK, MCP server, agent card or machine-readable API contract. This file was generated by API Evangelist from the company's public website; it is not published by Renew Financial. Do not infer a callable API surface from it.

## How the product works

- PACE financing is repaid through a voluntary assessment on the property tax bill, secured by a lien, with fixed rates and terms up to 30 years.
- Qualification is based on income and home equity rather than a FICO threshold.
- Funds are released to the contractor only after the homeowner signs a completion certificate.
- Financed categories include solar, HVAC, roofing, windows and doors, landscaping and turf, hurricane protection, and seismic retrofit.
